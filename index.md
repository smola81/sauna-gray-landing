## Track every sauna session with confidence

SaunaGray transforms your Garmin smartwatch into a comprehensive sauna monitoring companion. Get real-time tracking, safety alerts, and detailed analytics to optimize your wellness routine while staying safe.

## Key features

- Live temperature display (°C) with color-coded zones
- Continuous heart rate monitoring (bpm)
- Smart alerts for extended sessions and high temperature/heart rate
- Milestone notifications every 6 minutes with vibration
- Multi-session tracking with automatic cooling period monitoring
- Detailed history, personal records, and session analytics

## Safety first

SaunaGray uses clear visual warnings and thresholds to help you stay within safe limits. Alerts trigger for extended sessions, temperature spikes, and elevated heart rate so you can focus on the experience.

## Compatibility

Fenix 6/6 Pro/6S/6S Pro/6X Pro, Fenix 7/7 Pro/7S/7S Pro/7X, Fenix 8 (43mm, 47/51mm, Pro, Solar), Epix 2/Pro, Venu 2/2 Plus, Forerunner 165/255/265/570/955/965/970, Descent G2/Mk3.
