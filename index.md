## SaunaGray - Professional Sauna Session Tracker

Transform your Garmin smartwatch into a comprehensive sauna monitoring companion. SaunaGray provides real-time tracking, safety alerts, and detailed analytics to help you optimize your wellness routine while staying safe.

## Key features

### Real-time monitoring

- Live temperature display (°C) with color-coded zones
- Continuous heart rate monitoring (bpm)
- Visual progress indicators and trend graphs

### Safety first

- Smart alerts for extended sessions (over 15min, over 18min, over 20min)
- Temperature warnings (over 80°C, over 85°C)
- Heart rate alerts (over 140 bpm, over 150 bpm)
- Color-coded visual warnings with severity indicators
- Milestone notifications every 6 minutes with vibration

### Multi-session tracking

- Track complete sauna activities with multiple sessions
- Automatic cooling period tracking between sessions
- Monitor temperature drop during recovery
- Cooling complete notification after 10 minutes

### Activity integration

- Automatic GPS location capture
- Save sessions directly to Garmin Connect and Strava
- Individual session recording with detailed metrics

### History & analytics

- View up to 50 past sessions with full details
- Session statistics: duration, max/min temperature, heart rate
- Activity-level and all-time statistics
- Personal records tracking
- Post-session temperature editing

### Smart visualizations

- Adaptive layouts optimized for different screen sizes
- Color-coded temperature zones (Cool/Warm/Hot/Very Hot)
- Heart rate zone indicators (Resting/Normal/Elevated/High)
- Mini trend graphs for temperature and heart rate
