How a session works

Sauna

Live timer, temperature and heart rate with zones. The ring fills to 15 min.

Cool

Cooling break timer and temperature drop. Buzzes when you're ready for the next round.

Save

Summary of rounds, max temp, average HR and calories. Saves to Garmin Connect and Strava.

Safety first

  • Alerts at 15, 18 and 20 minutes in the sauna
  • Temperature warnings over 80°C and 85°C
  • Heart rate alerts over 140 and 150 bpm
  • Vibration and a full-screen alert so you never miss one

Features

Multi-round tracking

Track complete sauna activities across multiple rounds, with automatic cooling in between.

History of 50 sessions

Look back at duration, max/min temperature and heart rate for your last 50 sessions.

Personal records

Longest session, most rounds and more, tracked automatically.

GPS location

Automatic GPS capture so your session shows where it happened.

Temperature editing

Adaptive layouts

Tuned for every supported screen size, from the smallest MIP display to large AMOLED.
