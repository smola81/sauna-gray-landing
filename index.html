SaunaGray

Professional sauna session tracker for Garmin smartwatches.

Track every sauna session with confidence

Real-time monitoring, safety alerts, and session analytics that help you stay safe and improve your routine.

Key features

  • Live temperature display (°C) with color-coded zones
  • Continuous heart rate monitoring (bpm)
  • Smart alerts for extended sessions and high temperature/heart rate
  • Milestone notifications every 6 minutes with vibration
  • Multi-session tracking with automatic cooling monitoring
  • Detailed history, personal records, and session analytics

Safety first

SaunaGray keeps you within safe limits with clear visual warnings, temperature thresholds, and heart rate alerts. Focus on the experience while the watch handles the monitoring.

Alerts trigger for extended sessions, temperature spikes, and elevated heart rate with color-coded feedback and vibration cues.

History & analytics

Review up to 50 past sessions with detailed stats: duration, max/min temperature, and heart rate. Track personal records and view trends over time.

SaunaGray saves activities to Garmin Connect and Strava and captures GPS automatically.

Compatibility

Fenix 6/6 Pro/6S/6S Pro/6X Pro, Fenix 7/7 Pro/7S/7S Pro/7X, Fenix 8 (43mm, 47/51mm, Pro, Solar), Epix 2/Pro, Venu 2/2 Plus, Forerunner 165/255/265/570/955/965/970, Descent G2/Mk3.
